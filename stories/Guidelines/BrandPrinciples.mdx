# Brand Principles

Crate expresses one personality for Cargoz, a **B2B logistics** product:

> **Modern · Professional · Mature**

These aren't decoration. They are the tie-breakers for every design decision. When two
options both "work," pick the one that is more restrained, clearer, and more confident.

---

## What each word means here

### Modern
Geometric Manrope headings, crisp flat surfaces, generous whitespace, a clean grid, and a
**single** restrained accent (teal). Modern is achieved by removing, not adding.

### Professional
High contrast, unmistakable hierarchy, and **predictable, consistent** components. Every
listing card, every form field behaves the same way. Trust signals (verified badges,
prices, phone/WhatsApp) are structural, not afterthoughts. Nothing is decorative for its
own sake.

### Mature
Confident **neutrals dominate** the interface. The accent is used sparingly and with
intent. Elevation and motion are subtle — never flashy. The system reads as something a
Coca-Cola or Noon operations manager already trusts.

---

## The rules these produce

**Color**
- Neutrals (the `text` ramp) carry the UI. The teal `primary` accent appears **only** on:
  primary actions, links, active/selected states, and focus rings.
- ✅ Use `--crate-color-action` for the one main action on a view.
- 🚫 Don't color multiple elements with the accent to "add life" — that reads junior.
- The decorative ramps (`coral`, `mint`, `soft-violet`, `yellow`, `sky-blue`) are for
  **data visualization and category coding only** — never buttons, links, or chrome.
- Status colors (`danger`, `success`, `warning`, `info`) communicate state, nothing else.

**Shape & depth**
- **Subtle radius** (`--crate-radius-md`, 6px) on controls and cards. Not pill-shaped, not
  sharp.
- **Border-led separation.** Resting surfaces use a 1px `--crate-color-border` and little
  or no shadow. Shadows (`shadow-md`+) are reserved for floating UI: dropdowns, modals,
  toasts.

**Type**
- Utilitarian scale — information first. Headings are sized to organize, not to shout.
- **16px body floor**, always. High contrast for Windows LCD glare.

**Motion** (Phase 2+)
- Quick and purposeful (150–200ms, ease-out). State feedback, not entertainment. No
  bounce, no long flourishes.

**Icons** (Phase 2+)
- Consistent stroke/outline style, geometric, 1.5px. Text-labeled `Button` is the default
  for actions; for compact, obvious actions use `IconButton` with a `label` tooltip so its
  purpose is still discoverable on hover.

---

## A quick gut check

Before shipping a screen, ask: _Would this look at home in an enterprise logistics tool a
40-year-old operations manager relies on every day?_ If it feels playful, busy, or
trend-chasing, pull it back.
